## 2026-10-26 - Opt-in seeded projection in toJsonEvent (senpi#4349, part of #4272)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/json-event.ts`: adds the `toJsonEvent(event, { blockSeeds: 1 })` overload. It projects
  `message_update` from the producer-time capture only (S02a): start seeds (`seed.block`, `coveredCodeUnits`, tool
  `rawArgumentPrefix`/`parsedThrough`), deltas with `offset` (and `parsedThrough` for tool arguments), ends with the frozen
  block, `done`/`error` with the terminal message, plus frozen `usage` and changed `messageMetadata`. Assistant
  `message_start`/`message_end` take the capture's frozen message. A record with no capture has no seeded form
  (`undefined`), so no seed is ever read from the live partial; identity fields are passed through, never minted.
  The no-options call is unchanged byte for byte; `print-mode.ts` and `rpc-mode.ts` keep it.

### Why

- R3.C6.2: a seed read from the shared live partial at serialization time can already contain later bytes, and an
  ordinary delta must not copy or reserialize a growing block.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The capture carriers are internal to the provider/agent pipeline and are not exposed to extension events.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `toJsonEvent` overload list in `json-event.ts`.

## 2026-10-26 - AgentSession structured lane (senpi#4349, part of #4272)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `subscribeStructured(listener)` exposes the internal structured
  lane. `_emit` forwards each published session event, with the capture of the agent event it came from, after the
  ordinary listeners; block controls from the agent's internal channel are queued on `_agentEventQueue`, so they keep
  their position among the session's events. Nothing is attached by default.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/structured-session-lane.ts` (new): owns the internal-channel subscription for one
  session; it holds the capture subscription only while a structured listener exists.

### Why

- R3.C6.2 placement ruling: S02b owns the bridge from the ordered internal-only agent channel to the S01 structured
  sink, attached before a turn's first provider push; controls never reach extension listeners or `session.subscribe`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Extensions see the public event stream only; the internal channel and capture carriers are deliberately not part of it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `_emit` (one trailing line and its `captureSource` parameter), the `_emitSessionEvent` call in
  `_processAgentEvent`, the constructor's lane construction, `dispose`.

## 2026-10-26 - Structured lane into the shared binding behind the compact-updates flag (senpi#4349, part of #4272)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/structured-session-records.ts` (new): `structuredLaneEnabled()` reads
  `<brand>_ENGINE_SOCKET_COMPACT_UPDATES` (explicit `1`/`true` only). `subscribeStructuredSessionRecords` correlates the
  lane like legacy records and gates blocks: a block's records are admitted only after its own seed or insert reached
  this subscriber (a late attach gets nothing for that message); a block whose seed was seen but whose next record or
  end has no capture is invalid, and the assistant `message_end` lists it in `invalidatedBlocks`. A terminal capture's
  `rewrittenFrom` (#4307 fold) invalidates every admitted block at or above that index, and a `message_end` with no
  interpretable terminal capture (cancel, idle timeout, a stream that ended without a terminal event, or a capture
  with no terminal message) invalidates every block of
  the message. Blocks are addressed by `capture.contentIndex ?? event.contentIndex`, never a drifted public index.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/connection-handler.ts`: optional `RpcConnectionSink.writeStructured`; when present
  the handler subscribes the structured lane next to the legacy subscription and tears it down with it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-binding.ts`: supplies `writeStructured` only while the flag is on, into
  `SessionEventWriter.structuredLanes` within the provider scope; closed scopes drop it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-structured-lanes.ts` (new), `session-event-writer.ts`: per-session
  receivers for the structured lane; legacy sockets, stdout and the replay snapshot never read it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-record-stream.ts`: `subscribeSessionRecords` takes an optional event source
  so both lanes share one correlation implementation.

### Why

- R3.C6.2 / ruling 05 D4: the three internal controls travel on the same ordered lane as ordinary records and never
  reach a legacy destination; flag off is byte-identical.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The binding and writer are host internals below extension delivery.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `installSessionSubscriptions` and `dispose` in `connection-handler.ts`; the `taggedSink` literal in
  `session-binding.ts`; the `subscribeSessionRecords` signature.

## 2026-10-26 - Reader note on the capture index (senpi#4349, part of #4272)

### What changed
